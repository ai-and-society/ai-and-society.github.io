A community-sourced list of AI & society resources on campus, including organizations, courses, and event series, compiled from suggestions by our members. If you would like to add a resource to this list, please contact the [organizing team](/organizers/).

### Organizations, centers, and initiatives

* [Berkeley Algorithms, Data, and Society (ADS)](https://tinyurl.com/berkeleyads): community of Berkeley CS/EECS researchers whose work considers the effects of algorithms on societal problems and vice versa — automated decision systems, algorithmic fairness and justice, and the repercussions of data collection and algorithmic interventions
* [BASIS — Berkeley AI Safety Initiative](https://berkeleyaisafety.com/): a community of students interested in building robust, transparent, and controllable AI systems
* [BASIS AI Policy Group](https://berkeleyaisafety.com/policy-group): introduces students to the political frameworks and interventions being explored to shape the future of AI development
* [Responsible & Equitable AI Initiative (RE-AI)](https://re-ai.berkeley.edu/home): an initiative housed in BAIR driving critical research, innovation, and collaboration towards responsible AI
* [Berkeley AI Research (BAIR)](https://bair.berkeley.edu/): campus AI research lab
* [UC Berkeley AI Community](https://ai.berkeley.edu/uc-berkeley-ai-community): campus-wide AI community and mailing list
* [AFOG — Algorithmic Fairness and Opacity Group](https://afog.berkeley.edu/): interdisciplinary working group on algorithmic fairness and opacity
* [BESI technology network](https://besi.berkeley.edu/research/technology/): the technology research network of the Berkeley Economy and Society Initiative
* [Simons Institute for the Theory of Computing](https://simons.berkeley.edu/): theory of computing institute; hosts AI/law fellows
* [Berkeley Center for Law & Technology (BCLT)](https://www.law.berkeley.edu/bclt): law and technology center; Berkeley Law also offers an emphasis and curriculum on AI policy
* [Berkeley Technology Law Journal (BTLJ)](https://btlj.org/): student-run technology law journal
* [Global Rights Innovation Lab Clinic (GRIL)](https://www.law.berkeley.edu/experiential/clinics/global-rights-innovation-lab-clinic/): a multidisciplinary client-driven lab clinic exploring the intersection of human rights advocacy and digital technologies
* [Script Encoding Initiative (SEI)](https://sei.berkeley.edu/): research in the Department of Linguistics toward encoding historic and modern minority scripts in the Unicode Standard, advancing digitization of the world's writing systems
* [Center for Science, Technology, Medicine & Society (CSTMS)](https://cstms.berkeley.edu/): research center hosting the STS graduate Designated Emphasis and undergraduate minor
* Human Technology Futures (HTF): proposed department for CDSS; website forthcoming
* [Berkeley Center for New Media (BCNM)](https://bcnm.berkeley.edu/): an interdisciplinary research center that studies and shapes media transition and emergence, cultivating technological equity and fairness in classrooms, communities, and on the internet
* [Computational Precision Health (CPH)](https://cph.berkeley.edu/): joint UC Berkeley–UCSF program in computational precision health
* [PIT-UN — Public Interest Technology University Network](https://pit-un.org/): network of universities building the public interest technology field
* [CITRIS Tech Policy Initiative](https://citris-uc.org/): organizes AI and tech policy events across the CITRIS campuses
* [D-Lab](https://dlab.berkeley.edu/): social science data lab offering training and community
* AI Risk Symposium: campus symposium and reading groups on AI risk
* [ML@Berkeley](https://ml.berkeley.edu/): student-run machine learning nonprofit
* Berkeley Institute of Design (BiD) Seminar Series: seminar series where many talks concern HCI in relation to AI
* [Stiles Hall](https://stileshall.org/): non-profit organization serving Berkeley students

### Courses

* [Data 104 — Human Contexts and Ethics of Data](https://data104.org/): backbone Data Science undergraduate course on data science, algorithmic decision systems, AI, and society
* SOCIOL 273L and SOCIOL 273M: graduate courses on computational methods in social science
* [SOCIOL 190-007](https://classes.berkeley.edu/index.php/content/2025-spring-sociol-190-007-sem-007): undergraduate seminar on the societal impacts and implications of AI
* EDUC C260: machine learning in education
* Law and Governance of AI: Berkeley Law course
* The Ethics of AI: student-led DeCal

### Speaker series and public programs

* [Coffee Break with Irene Liu](https://executive.law.berkeley.edu/boost-show/coffee-break-with-irene-liu/): free Berkeley Law webinar series that often addresses AI-related topics
* [Sunrise Lectures on Film and Media](https://german.berkeley.edu/about/projects-lectures/sunrise-lecture-media-and-technology): German Department lecture series that also covers [AI and culture](https://artshumanities.berkeley.edu/news/post-artificial-writing-professor-hannes-bajohr-ai-authorship-and-future-literature)
* [Deb Donig](https://www.ischool.berkeley.edu/people/deb-donig): I School faculty lecturer speaking and organizing on AI and ethics
